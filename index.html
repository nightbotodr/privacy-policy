Privacy Policy

Application: Color Ball Adventure
Package Name: com.color_bal_adventure
Effective Date: October 12, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Color Ball Adventure ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles your information. We are fully dedicated to protecting the privacy of our players. Color Ball Adventure does not collect, sell, track, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do NOT collect personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not collect precise or approximate geolocation data.
  • We do not access camera, microphone, contacts, biometric data, or external storage files.
  • We do not track user behavior across other apps or websites.

3. Local Device Data Storage

Color Ball Adventure stores gameplay progress strictly on your device using local storage (HTML5 LocalStorage / client-side app cache). This includes:

  • Highest unlocked level (Levels 1 to 50).
  • Earned star ratings per level.
  • Total in-game coins collected.
  • Unlocked and equipped ball skins.
  • Audio settings (sound effects and music toggles).

This information is stored exclusively on your local device to enable game persistence between sessions. It is never uploaded to any external server or shared with third parties. You can clear this data at any time through the in-game "Reset All Progress" button or by clearing the app's cache and data in your Android system settings.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The game does not integrate any third-party behavioral advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or data brokers.

5. Device Permissions

Color Ball Adventure requires only minimal standard permissions:

  • Network/Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Used exclusively to facilitate local runtime web engine rendering and standard applet execution. No personal information is transmitted.

We do not request sensitive runtime permissions such as Location, Storage scanning, Contacts, Camera, or Audio recording.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Color Ball Adventure is designed as a family-friendly casual platformer suitable for audiences of all ages, including children under 13 (and under 16 in the European Union).

In strict compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect, request, or retain personal data from children.
  • The game contains no behavioral tracking, interest-based advertisements, or social chat features.

If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take immediate steps to delete any such data.

7. Security

We value your trust. Because Color Ball Adventure does not collect or transmit personal information over the internet, your personal data is not at risk of server breaches or unauthorized remote disclosure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal regulations. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
